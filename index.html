NIRIKSHAN AI is a student concept for a web-based integrated infrastructure project-monitoring platform. It helps demonstrate how monitoring teams could identify potential cost, schedule, and implementation risks early.

Current demo

Responsive UI, sample project data, interactive filters, early warnings, explainable risk factors, and rule-based assistant responses.
